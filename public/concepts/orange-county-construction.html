A few good questions

Big ideas.
Clearer answers.

Should I convert my garage or build a new ADU?

That depends on the existing structure, available space and how you plan to use it. The first conversation should compare those options for your property—not assume one is right.

What will my project cost?

Size, site conditions, utilities and finishes all affect the scope. Ask for a property-specific written estimate rather than relying on a generic price online.

Do I need finished plans to get in touch?

You can start with an idea and your city. The initial discussion can identify what information is needed next.

Can you tell me what my property allows?

Property-specific restrictions and current local requirements need review. This design concept does not provide permit, zoning or legal advice.
