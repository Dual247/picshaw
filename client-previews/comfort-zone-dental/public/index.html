BEFORE YOU COME IN

A few
helpful details.

Where is Comfort Zone Dental?

The office is at 3808 W Riverside Drive, Suite 508, Burbank, CA 91505. Call (818) 955-9788 if you need help finding the practice.

When is the office open?

The current website lists Tuesday through Thursday, 9 am to 5 pm. Please call to confirm appointment availability.

What if I feel nervous about visiting?

The practice places an emphasis on patient comfort. Let the team know when you call so you can discuss your concerns before the visit.

How do I check my insurance?

Contact the office with your plan details before booking. Coverage and any out-of-pocket costs should be confirmed with the practice and your insurer.

Can I book through this preview?

This is a proposed website design, so the sample form does not send information or book an appointment. For a real enquiry, please telephone the practice.
