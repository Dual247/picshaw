A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before
your visit.

Where is the office?

2103 Montrose Avenue, Suite A, Montrose, CA 91020. Call (818) 248-1718 if you need help finding the office.

Are evening or Saturday visits available?

The published hours include Tuesday and Thursday evenings until 9 pm, and Saturdays from 9 am to 3 pm. Contact the team to confirm current appointment availability.

Does the practice see children?

Yes. The practice’s published services include children’s dentistry as well as general, cosmetic, restorative and orthodontic care for adults.

How can I check my insurance?

Call the office with your plan details before your visit. The team can help you check the information needed for your appointment.

Will this preview book an appointment?
