CLEAR ANSWERS.

Can I start with photos of the damage?

Yes. Expo’s existing website accepts up to four photos with an estimate request. Photos help start the conversation; a complete assessment may require a visit.

Can you help with my insurance company?

Expo handles communication with insurance companies as part of its repair service. Talk to the team about your claim and what information they need.

Do you handle small dents and paint damage?

Expo’s services include dent removal, bodywork, paint matching and collision repairs. Share what you’ve noticed so the team can advise on the next step.

What if I need towing?

Expo lists towing among its services. Call (310) 615-0155 to discuss your vehicle and the available arrangements.
